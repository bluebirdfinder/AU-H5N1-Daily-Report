🕊️ Silver Gull (Chroicocephalus novaehollandiae) 🔴 High Risk Vector
Status: Resident / Coastal Scavenger DAFF Detections: 67 (13%)

Habitats & Ecology: Omnivorous, highly adapted to human urban environments, ports, landfills, and open dining areas. Densely gregarious.

Bio-Security Assessment: Frequent bridge vector between wild wetlands and urban/feed areas. Primary biosecurity monitoring target.

🪶 Crested Tern (Thalasseus bergii) 🟠 Mass Outbreak Risk
Status: Coastal Nomadic Seabird DAFF Detections: 365 (71%)

Habitats & Ecology: Piscivorous, nests in dense breeding colonies of thousands on offshore islands and sandbars.

Bio-Security Assessment: Prone to mass mortality events in coastal breeding colonies (SA & VIC coastlines), but rarely strays inland.

🌊 Giant Petrel (Macronectes giganteus) 🟡 Moderate Coastal Risk
Status: Sub-Antarctic Oceanic Migrant DAFF Detections: 57 (11%)

Habitats & Ecology: Large pelagic seabird following fishing vessels and scavenging on oceanic waters.

Bio-Security Assessment: High pelagic range, susceptible to carrying virus via scavenging infected seabirds.

🦤 Pacific Gull (Larus pacificus) 🟡 Moderate Coastal Risk
Status: Southern AU Endemic Seabird DAFF Detections: 4 (0.8%)

Habitats & Ecology: Large endemic gull inhabiting rocky coasts and beaches, feeding on crabs and fish.

Bio-Security Assessment: Confined strictly to coastal shorelines, rarely strays inland.

🦅 Brown Skua (Stercorarius antarcticus) 🟡 Long-Range Carrier
Status: Sub-Antarctic Pelagic Migrant DAFF Detections: 3 (0.6%)

Habitats & Ecology: Aggressive predatory and scavenging pelagic seabird. Capable of long-distance transoceanic flights.

Bio-Security Assessment: Carries Antarctic/Sub-Antarctic strains north to southern Australian offshore islands (e.g. Rocky Cape, TAS).

🐧 Little Penguin (Eudyptula minor) 🟢 Low Inland Risk
Status: Coastal Resident / Diving Seabird DAFF Detections: 1 (0.2%)

Habitats & Ecology: Inhabits southern Australian coastlines and offshore islands (e.g. Phillip Island). Flightless, nocturnal burrower.

Bio-Security Assessment: Strictly marine and coastal habitat. Flightless, primary role is susceptible sentinel host.

Avian Telemetry & Sighting Source Decryption

💡 4-Pillar Avian Telemetry Guide: Which is Closest to Real Arrival?

Understanding seasonal models, live eBird field sightings, Movebank satellite GPS, and deduplicated GBIF research datasets

🎯 Decision-Maker Guide: Which Metric Should You Trust?
1. "How many birds actually landed?" 👉 Check eBird Live Sightings (Verified citizen & expert eyeball observations on the ground).
2. "Where are the flocks now?" 👉 Check Movebank Satellite GPS (Live telemetry transmitters tracking flight vectors towards NSW).
3. "What is the seasonal peak?" 👉 Check Historical Seasonal Model (2.0M baseline evaluating multi-wave arrival progress).

🕊️ 1. Historical Seasonal Carrying Capacity Model (EAAF)

Mathematical Model

Meaning: Mathematical model derived from BirdLife Australia's 35,000+ national wetland surveys. Projects Australia's annual theoretical peak (~2.0M - 2.2M birds) and monthly influx stages (~25% in Sep, 100% in Oct).

Nature: Macro theoretical baseline representing historical expectations, not live daily tally.

🦅 2. eBird Live Field Sightings ⭐ Closest to Ground Reality

Ground Truth

Meaning: Real-time eyeball observations and verified headcounts reported by professional ornithologists and rangers across Australian wetlands and coastlines.

Nature: Highest fidelity ground-truth metric (e.g. 1,407 birds across 129 hotspots; 286 birds in NSW).

🛰️ 3. Movebank Satellite GPS Telemetry

Live Flight Vectors

Meaning: Solar-powered satellite GPS transmitters fitted on key species (Short-tailed Shearwater, Bar-tailed Godwit) transmitting trans-oceanic flight coordinates via Argos/Iridium.

Nature: Early-warning radar tracking whether vanguards have crossed the equator and entered NSW airspace.

🌐 4. GBIF Deduplicated Academic Research Data

Deduplicated Science

Meaning: Academic surveys from CSIRO research vessels, state agencies, and museums. "Deduplicated" means our pipeline automatically filters out duplicate entries and overlapping eBird datasets to keep academic data robust and unbiased.

Nature: Covers remote wilderness and open-ocean zones not frequently surveyed by citizen birders.
